Ask another model a question about one video. n_frames frames are taken at evenly spaced times from video_start_sec to video_end_sec, including both ends, at the original resolution. The model sees only these frames, each labeled with its time, and no audio. Its answer is returned.

video: the video number, "1" to "23".
video_start_sec, video_end_sec: start and end of the range in seconds within that video; every video starts at 0.
n_frames: the number of frames, at most 60.
question: the question to ask about the frames.
