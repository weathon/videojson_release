Answer the question from the video index in /data. The video is a collection of 23 livestream videos, numbered 1 to 23 in recording order, about 105 hours in total. The index is eight JSON files; video in them is the video number as a string ("1" to "23"), and every time is HH:MM:SS within that video (every video starts at 00:00:00).

/data/captions_30sec.json, /data/captions_3min.json, /data/captions_10min.json and /data/captions_1h.json are JSON lists of chronological captions of 30-second, 3-minute, 10-minute and one-hour segments; each record has video, start_time, end_time and text. /data/episodic_triples.json is a JSON list with one record for each 30-second caption: video, start_time, end_time and triples, the [subject, relation, object] triples of what happens in that caption. /data/semantic_triples.json is a JSON list with one record per video: video and triples, the [subject, relation, object] triples of general knowledge inferred from the episodic triples of that video and consolidated over time: relationships and roles, attributes and preferences, habits and capabilities, and conceptual knowledge. /data/topic_chains.json is a JSON list of entities, each with entity and facts, the facts about that entity over all the videos, each with video, time and fact. /data/event_chains.json is a JSON list of activities that run over several steps, each with video, name, type, key_entities and steps, each step with start_time, end_time and description.
