Answer the question from the video index in /data. The video is one livestream video of about 23.6 hours. The index is eight JSON files; every time in them is HH:MM:SS within the video, which starts at 00:00:00.

/data/captions_30sec.json, /data/captions_3min.json, /data/captions_10min.json and /data/captions_1h.json are JSON lists of chronological captions of 30-second, 3-minute, 10-minute and one-hour segments; each record has start_time, end_time and text. /data/episodic_triples.json is a JSON list with one record for each 30-second caption: start_time, end_time and triples, the [subject, relation, object] triples of what happens in that caption. /data/semantic_triples.json is a JSON list of [subject, relation, object] triples of general knowledge inferred from the episodic triples and consolidated over time: relationships and roles, attributes and preferences, habits and capabilities, and conceptual knowledge. /data/topic_chains.json is a JSON list of entities, each with entity and facts, the facts about that entity, each with time and fact. /data/event_chains.json is a JSON list of activities that run over several steps, each with name, type, key_entities and steps, each step with start_time, end_time and description.
