Answer the question from the video index in /data. The video is the first-person footage of {name}, who wears the camera, over several days; the questions are asked by {name}, so I, me and my refer to {name}. The user message gives the day and the time at which the question is asked. The index is eight JSON files; every time in them is day (DAY1 to DAY7) and wall-clock time of that day.

/data/captions_30sec.json, /data/captions_3min.json, /data/captions_10min.json and /data/captions_1h.json are JSON lists of chronological captions of 30-second, 3-minute, 10-minute and one-hour segments; each record has day, start_time and end_time (HH:MM:SS) and text. /data/episodic_triples.json is a JSON list with one record for each 30-second caption: day, start_time, end_time and triples, the [subject, relation, object] triples of what happens in that caption. /data/semantic_triples.json is a JSON list of [subject, relation, object] triples of general knowledge inferred from the episodic triples and consolidated over time: relationships and roles, attributes and preferences, habits and capabilities, and conceptual knowledge. /data/topic_chains.json is a JSON list of entities, each with entity and facts, the facts about that entity, each with time (DAY<n> HH:MM) and fact. /data/event_chains.json is a JSON list of activities that run over several steps, each with name and steps, each step with day, start_time, end_time (HH:MM:SS) and description.
