Ask a visual question about an interval of a video. video is the video number of the index record (for example 3); start_s and end_s are seconds within that video, as tick_start_s and tick_end_s in the record. Frames are sampled at fps frames per second and answered by a separate vision model; the images do not enter your context. fps can be at most 0.5. The interval can be at most 20 minutes and at most 650 frames. Returns status (answered or unknown), the answer and the times of the evidence frames.
